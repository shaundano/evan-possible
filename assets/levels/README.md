# Level media: sources

Still missing (game runs without it): classroom guy art for beatbox (currently CSS/emoji; beat noises are synthesized in code).

- `shaggs-who-are-parents.mp3` - 18s excerpt (from 0:20) of "Who Are Parents?", trimmed from a user-supplied copy
- `whomst-ve.mp3` - macOS `say -v Daniel -r 120 "Whomst've"`
- `garfield.png` - old (1980s) Garfield art supplied by the user (`assets/design-ref/garfield-old.png`), white background flood-filled to transparent
- `pipe.png` - the pipe from Magritte "The Treachery of Images" (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:MagrittePipe.jpg, non-free, private use), cropped and background made transparent
- `poster-*.jpg` - theatrical posters from each film's English Wikipedia infobox (non-free, used for identification only; private use)
- `elevator-music.mp3` - "Local Forecast - Elevator" by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), CC BY 4.0, first 60s with fades; looped in the movie night level. Credit: Kevin MacLeod, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- `../sounds/yay.mp3` - first 4s of "Clapping hurray (cropped)" by Starlite, public domain: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Clapping_hurray_(cropped).oga
- `../sounds/bgm.mp3` - game background music, "Gonna Fly Now" (Bill Conti) from a user-supplied copy, re-encoded at 128k. Engine loops it at volume .25 after the first click; it is paused during the movie night question (elevator music) and resumed after. (If `1-pages-and-assets` is merged too, its extracted SWF `bgm.mp3` conflicts with this one: keep this one.)
